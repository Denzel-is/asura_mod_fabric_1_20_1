# Magic Staff - Quick Start Guide

## How to Get the Staff

### Method 1: Creative Mode (Easiest)
1. Open creative inventory (`E`)
2. Search for "**Asura Mod**" tab OR "staff"
3. Click on the **Staff** item
4. Drag it to your inventory

### Method 2: Craft It (Survival)

**Recipe:**
```
  A    (A = Aether Quartz Shard)
 S     (S = Stormsilver Ingot)
S
```

**Shapedpattern:**
```
[ ][ ][A]
[ ][S][ ]
[S][ ][ ]
```

**Materials needed:**
- 1x Aether Quartz Shard
- 2x Stormsilver Ingot

## How to Use

### Holding the Staff
- **Left-click** repeatedly = melee attack (like any weapon)
- **Right-click once** = cast spell (if imprinted)

### Imprinting a Spell
1. Get a **Spellbook** (also in creative menu or craft it)
2. Open the Spellbook UI (right-click with spellbook)
3. Design your spell in the graph editor
4. Save/imprint the spell to the staff

### Visual Features
✓ **Trident-like pose** when held (upright angle)
✓ **Cannot be thrown** (no projectile)
✓ **Charge bar** shows spell charges (bottom of icon)
✓ **Tooltip** shows imprinted spell name

## In-Game Location

The staff appears in:
- **Custom "Asura Mod" creative tab** (main location)
- Search bar: type "staff"

No console commands needed!

## Crafting Materials Guide

### Where to get materials:

**Aether Quartz Shard:**
- Mine Aether Quartz Ore (Overworld, any level)
- Mine Deepslate Aether Quartz Ore (deep underground)

**Stormsilver Ingot:**
- Smelt Stormsilver Ore in furnace
- Combine 9x Stormsilver Nuggets

**Quick path:** 
Play in creative mode → Get materials from Asura Mod tab → Craft staff
